Professional introduction

Building, documenting, and sharing what I learn.

My work brings together software, cybersecurity, communication, and community service.

I have developed web projects with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, MySQL, and JSON; built focused Python and security learning labs; and written articles about Python, databases, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and developer tools.

Community work has made the technical work more concrete. I contributed more than 270 hours over three years to the Community Book Exchange, supported TELUS World of Science summer camps, presented at CANEFOR 2025, and volunteered with the BSides Edmonton 2025 Social Media Team.
